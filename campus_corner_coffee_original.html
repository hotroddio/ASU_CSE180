FAQ

A few quick answers

When are you open?

Official business hours weren’t included in the customer dataset. Please check with the café for current opening and closing times.

Is Campus Corner a place to study?

Studying was the most common visit purpose in the sample, representing 71 of 180 recorded visits.

What should I try?

Cold Brew was the most frequently recorded individual product, followed by Green Tea and Muffin.

Can I use Wi-Fi?

Wi-Fi use was recorded for 97 visits. The dataset doesn’t confirm network availability or access details, so ask the café team.
